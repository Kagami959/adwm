Acoustic–Duration Watermarking

One message, carried by syllable timing and codec residuals.

Shared 8-bit payload · Complementary recovery · CRC-8 check

How it works

1Embed

The same 8-bit message P is embedded into the syllable-duration structure (mod-4) and the codec acoustic residuals (as [CRC8(P) | P], 16 bits).

2Attack

Different speech regeneration paths can retain different watermark cues. Syllable durations and codec residuals offer complementary recovery paths.

3Decode

The receiver checks the acoustic candidate using CRC-8 and a watermark-presence score. If those checks fail, it evaluates the duration candidate using extraction validity and confidence.

This page demonstrates the residual-domain step (VoiceMark-compatible, transcript-free). The complementary duration-domain carrier is applied at TTS synthesis time and is exercised in the paper’s full evaluation.
